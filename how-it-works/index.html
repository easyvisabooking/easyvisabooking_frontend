How Our US Visa Appointment Booking Service Works

Our US visa appointment booking process is straightforward and transparent. Whether you are in Dubai, Canada, or anywhere worldwide, here is exactly what happens after you contact us, from your first message through to a confirmed interview date.

Our Process

Step-by-Step Booking Process

Step 01
You Share Your Details
You send us your DS-160 reference number, your preferred consulate, and the date range that works for your travel plans. Let us know if you prefer a morning or afternoon interview and we will factor that in. The wider the range you can accept, the more often we can move your date, so tell us the full window your travel plans allow rather than a single preferred day. Everything you share is handled confidentially and used only to arrange your appointment. We will confirm exactly what is required, and our service fee, in writing before any work begins.
DS-160 reference Preferred dates Fee agreed in writing
Step 02
We Review Your Case
A coordinator reviews your profile against the requirements of the relevant scheduling portal, AIS or CGI, depending on your visa category and consulate location. We check that your DS-160 is complete, that your MRV fee is paid and that a placeholder appointment exists, since without one the portal will not allow rescheduling to an earlier date. We flag anything missing before we begin.
AIS portal CGI portal Eligibility check
Step 03
Ongoing Appointment Support
Our team takes over the day-to-day work of tracking availability for your consulate and date range, so you are not checking the portal yourself or setting alarms. Earlier dates typically come from cancellations by other applicants, which are unpredictable and outside anyone's control. We keep you updated on progress and tell you honestly if your requested range looks unrealistic.
Handled for you Regular updates Honest guidance
Step 04
Appointment Confirmed
When an appointment within your requested range becomes available, we secure it through the official scheduling portal and confirm it with you by email and WhatsApp, along with the official confirmation for your records. Our service fee becomes payable at this point and not before. You can then focus on preparing for your interview.
Official portal Email confirmation Fee due on success

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions from our clients

How soon can I get an urgent appointment?

We assist with B1/B2, H1B, F1, J1, L1 and all other visa categories. How quickly an earlier date becomes available depends on cancellation activity at your consulate, which varies week to week and is outside our control.

For current wait times at your specific embassy, visit: US State Department Global Visa Wait Times

Do you provide guaranteed dates?

No, and you should be cautious of any service that says otherwise. Appointment availability is controlled entirely by the consulate and by cancellations from other applicants. We cannot guarantee a specific interview date or timeline. What we do commit to is handling the scheduling process properly on your behalf, and charging our service fee only if we successfully secure an appointment for you.

Is my data secure and private?

Yes. All information you provide for your united states visa appointment is encrypted, confidential, and never shared with any third party. We prioritize your privacy and follow strict data-security protocols.

Do you book appointments worldwide?

Yes! We fully support both AIS & CGI systems globally, helping applicants from multiple countries secure their U.S. visa appointment dates. Our us visa scheduling system covers all major embassies and consulates worldwide.

Our global network includes major visa appointment booking in:

  • Canada
  • United Kingdom
  • Dubai
  • Turkey
  • India
  • European Union countries
  • And many more...
How and when am I charged?

Our service fee becomes payable only once we have successfully secured an appointment for you. There is no upfront service fee and no subscription.

The fee is confirmed with you in writing before any work begins and covers our scheduling assistance only. It is separate from the MRV visa fee, which you pay directly to the US Department of State and which we never collect on your behalf.

Full details are set out in our Refund and Cancellation Policy.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Easy Visa Booking collects, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have over it. It applies to our website and to the appointment scheduling assistance services we provide.

We collect the minimum information we need to arrange your appointment. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us directly

  • Contact details: your name, email address, WhatsApp or phone number, and country.
  • Appointment details: your DS-160 confirmation reference, visa category, preferred consulate, existing appointment date, and preferred date range.
  • Correspondence: the content of messages you send us by email, WhatsApp, Telegram or our website contact form.

What we never collect

We do not ask for, receive, or store your login credentials for the official visa scheduling portal. Any action requiring a portal sign-in is performed by you, on your own device, using your own credentials. See section 6 of our Terms of Service.

We do not collect or store your payment card details. Payments are handled by a third-party payment processor and your full card number is never transmitted to or stored on our systems.

You should never send your portal password to us or to anyone else. If you receive such a request claiming to be from us, it is not genuine, and we would be grateful if you reported it to contact@easyvisabooking.com.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, browser and device type, referring website, and time spent on pages.

3. Why We Use Your Information

  • To assess whether we can assist with your appointment and to confirm our fee to you.
  • To book or reschedule your US visa interview appointment through the official portal.
  • To communicate with you about your appointment and to send confirmations.
  • To take payment for our service and to keep accounting and tax records.
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To understand how our website is used so we can improve it.
  • To comply with our legal obligations and to detect or prevent fraud.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where data protection law such as the UK/EU GDPR or Canada's PIPEDA applies, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract: to deliver the service you have engaged us for.
  • Consent: for optional items such as analytics cookies and, where applicable, scheduling portal access. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate, secure and improve our business, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: to meet accounting, tax and anti-fraud requirements.

5. Who We Share Information With

We share personal information only with the following categories of recipient, and only as far as necessary:

  • The official US visa scheduling portal, in order to book or reschedule your appointment. This is inherent to the service.
  • Web3Forms, which processes submissions from our website contact form and delivers them to our email.
  • Google Analytics and Ahrefs Analytics, which provide aggregate website usage statistics.
  • Our payment processor, which handles payment transactions and card data directly.
  • Hosting and email providers that operate our website and communications infrastructure.
  • Professional advisers or authorities, where we are required to do so by law.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies. Strictly necessary cookies keep the site working. Analytics cookies, set by Google Analytics and Ahrefs Analytics, help us understand aggregate usage such as which pages are most read.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Blocking analytics cookies does not affect your ability to use our service.

7. How Long We Keep Your Information

  • Appointment and case information: retained while we are assisting you and for up to 12 months afterwards, to handle any follow-up queries or disputes.
  • Financial and transaction records: retained for as long as required by applicable tax and accounting law.
  • Website analytics data: retained in aggregate form according to the provider's standard retention period.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner. See section 9.

8. How We Protect Your Information

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, including encrypted transmission over HTTPS, access controls limiting who can see your information, and deletion of sensitive information once it is no longer needed. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take these obligations seriously and will notify you and any relevant regulator without undue delay if a breach affecting your information occurs.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete your personal information.
  • Object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing.
  • Request your information in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@easyvisabooking.com. We will respond within 30 days. We do not charge for this and we will not treat you differently for asking.

10. International Transfers

We assist applicants in several countries, and some of our service providers operate outside your country of residence. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we take steps to ensure it remains protected by appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent measures.

11. Children

Our service is directed at adults. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 16. Where an appointment is being arranged for a minor, we deal only with their parent or legal guardian, who is responsible for providing that information.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page shows when the current version took effect. Material changes will be communicated to active clients by email.

13. Contact Us

For any privacy question or request, contact contact@easyvisabooking.com. You can also reach us through the channels listed on our contact page.

1. Who We Are

Easy Visa Booking ("we", "us", "our") is a private, independent company providing appointment scheduling assistance to applicants for United States non-immigrant visas. Our registered details are set out at the top of this page and on our About page.

We are entirely independent. Official information about US visas is available free of charge at travel.state.gov. You are always free to book your own appointment directly through the official portal without using our service or paying us anything.

2. What Our Service Is

We provide administrative assistance with the scheduling of US visa interview appointments. This may include:

  • Reviewing your case to confirm you meet the prerequisites for booking or rescheduling.
  • Assisting you in booking a US visa interview appointment through the official scheduling portal.
  • Assisting you in rescheduling an existing appointment to an earlier available date, using the standard rescheduling function that the official portal makes available to every applicant.
  • Taking responsibility for tracking appointment availability for your chosen consulate and date range.
  • Notifying you when an appointment has been secured and providing the official confirmation.

All appointments are made exclusively through the official US visa scheduling portal. We do not use any unofficial, privileged or back-channel route, and we have no ability to obtain appointments that are not publicly available to other applicants.

3. What Our Service Is Not

To avoid any misunderstanding, our service expressly does not include the following, and you agree that you are not engaging us for any of them:

  • We do not provide legal or immigration advice. We are not lawyers, attorneys, or licensed immigration consultants. If you need advice on your immigration matter, you should consult a suitably qualified professional.
  • We do not influence visa decisions. Whether your visa is approved, refused or placed into administrative processing is determined solely by the consular officer. We have no involvement in, and no influence over, that decision.
  • We do not create appointment availability. We can only work with appointment dates that the consulate actually releases or that become available through cancellations by other applicants.
  • We do not complete your DS-160 or make any representations to the U.S. Government on your behalf.
  • We do not collect government fees. The Machine Readable Visa (MRV) fee and any other government charges are paid by you directly to the relevant authority through the official portal. We never collect, hold, process or refund those fees.

4. Eligibility and Prerequisites

Before we can assist you, the following must normally already be in place:

  • A completed and submitted DS-160 form, with the confirmation barcode number available.
  • The MRV fee paid in full, with a valid receipt.
  • An existing appointment already booked on the scheduling portal. This is required because the portal only offers the rescheduling function once an appointment exists.
  • A valid passport meeting the relevant validity requirements.

We will confirm the specific requirements for your case before any work begins. If you do not meet them, we will tell you rather than take your money.

6. Your Portal Login and Account Security

We do not ask for, receive, or store your login credentials for the official visa scheduling portal. Your portal account remains entirely under your own control at all times.

Any action that requires signing in to the official scheduling portal is carried out by you, on your own device, using your own credentials. We never hold them, and we have no independent means of accessing your portal account.

Because of this:

  • You retain sole control of your scheduling portal account at every stage.
  • You should never send your portal password to us, or to any other third party, by email, WhatsApp, Telegram or any other channel. If anyone claiming to represent us asks you for it, that request is not genuine and you should report it to contact@easyvisabooking.com.

How we handle the information you do share with us is set out in our Privacy Policy.
